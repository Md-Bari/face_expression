🌈 Emotion Spectrum Breakdown

😃

Happy (Joy, Contentment, Delight)

Zygomatic major muscle activation with genuine eye crinkles and smile curvature.

😲

Surprise (Awe, Startle, Shock)

Raised eyebrows, widened palpebral fissure, and jaw relaxation.

😐

Neutral (Calm, Composed, Baseline)

Resting facial muscle posture with balanced symmetry.

😢

Sad (Grief, Sorrow, Melancholy)

Depressor anguli oris pull, inner brow rise, and lowered gaze.

😠

Angry (Frustration, Rage, Irritation)

Corrugator brow knit, lowered inner brows, and tightened lips.

😨

Fear (Apprehension, Anxiety, Dread)

Tense lower eyelids, elevated upper eyelids, and lateral lip pull.

🤢

Disgust (Aversion, Distaste, Revulsion)

Levator labii superioris wrinkle on the nose bridge and curled upper lip.

😒

Contempt (Disdain, Scorn, Skepticism)

Unilateral lip corner tightening and slight asymmetric smirk.
